VERSION 12 — Mobile Sound + Seasonal 3D Preview

- iPhone sound: a single shared Web Audio context for splash, sound test and nature ambience. Audio is resumed synchronously during user taps; a two-tone sound test makes testing easier.
- Nature audio can be toggled without closing the shared context; avoids conflicts between audio systems.
- AR page: lightweight animated seasonal particles in the web 3D preview: spring petals, summer fireflies, autumn leaves and winter snow. Effects are drawn on a canvas over the model viewer, not embedded in the model.
- AR page: sound test button, light mode button, clearer fallback messaging.
- Native AR on iPhone Quick Look and Android Scene Viewer remains dependent on device/browser services. Native camera AR displays static USDZ/GLB models; webpage particles and audio cannot transfer into native AR.
- This release cannot make Honor 80 native AR work without supported AR services; use the animated 3D viewer fallback.

TEST:
1. Open index.html on iPhone Safari over HTTPS. Tap Enable / Test Sound and listen for tones + splash.
2. Open Sound & Quest > Enable audio; tap water to test splash.
3. Open ar.html; choose each season and watch the animated web preview.
4. On Honor 80, if AR is unavailable, use the interactive 3D preview. Test in the device browser.

Physical device tests have NOT been performed. Third-party CDNs still require network access.
