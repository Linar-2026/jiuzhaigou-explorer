iPhone 15 Pro Max: Open this page in Safari over HTTPS, wait for the model to load, then tap “Place landscape in AR.” If Quick Look cannot start, the device/browser may be unable to generate the required USDZ conversion; in that case this remains a working 3D preview, not confirmed iPhone AR. AR mode allows placement, rotation and scaling, but does not include the desktop information hotspots.
