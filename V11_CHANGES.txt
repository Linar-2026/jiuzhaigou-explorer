VERSION 11 — MOBILE COMPATIBILITY PATCH

Replace index.html and ar.html in the GitHub repository root. Keep all GLB and USDZ files unchanged.

Changes:
- Mobile sound test button; audio context is resumed before playing splash.
- Reduced mobile renderer resolution and disabled real-time shadows on small screens.
- Hides overlapping legacy floating quest/audio/discovery panels on narrow screens; primary adventure and bottom controls remain.
- AR page loads model-viewer from jsDelivr rather than Google AJAX CDN.
- AR page offers WebXR, Scene Viewer and Quick Look in supported environments, with 3D fallback messaging.
- AR model viewer uses no auto-rotate or real-time shadow.

LIMITATIONS:
- Native AR is device/browser dependent. Android AR may require Google Play Services for AR, which may be unavailable on some China-market phones.
- Third-party CDN access in mainland China can vary; for fully offline/China-reliable use, vendor model-viewer and Three.js assets locally.
- USDZ color/material rendering must be verified on actual iPhones.
- This update was syntax and ZIP-checked, not device-tested.
- The game itself has not been redesigned in this patch.
